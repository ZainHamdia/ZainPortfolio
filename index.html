Princeton Day School, Class of 2027 · Princeton, NJ

Hi, I’m Zain. I build robots, flight sim controls, and things my fire station actually uses.

I want to study mechanical engineering. Since 2019 I’ve been building the Zainiac robots. The first one was a five-foot humanoid made of wood, PVC, and LEGO Mindstorms that I took to Maker Faires in Brussels, Cairo, Philadelphia, and Rochester. The latest is a 3D-printed animatronic head that runs on seven servos.

I’m also a volunteer firefighter at Station 46 in Montgomery Township. There I built the radio room display and redid the department’s website.

Projects

  • Robotics · 2019 – 2025

    The Zainiac robots

    Three robots I built at home. Zainiac 19 is the five-foot humanoid I brought to Maker Faires and showed on Belgian national TV. Zainiac 25 (“Noggin”) is an animatronic head that’s entirely 3D-printed. The page also covers my VEX V5 work at Princeton Day School.

  • Fire Station 46 · 2025 – present

    Fire Station 46

    I volunteer as a firefighter and an engineering intern at Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Co. #2. I built the Raspberry Pi display in our radio room and designed its 3D-printed wall mount in CAD. I also rebuilt the department’s website.

  • Aviation

    Flight simulator controls

    A 3D-printed yoke that springs back to center on pitch and roll, plus a four-lever airliner throttle quadrant. Each lever has its own potentiometer.

    How it’s built
  • Princeton University LLP · Summer 2025

    A new switch thrower board for MAE 412

    Princeton’s model railroad switch thrower board was about 20 years old. I redesigned it as a single-sided PCB with an onboard ATmega328P and two 4700µF capacitors, then soldered it by hand and wrote a paper on it.
