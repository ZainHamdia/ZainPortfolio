Zainiac 19

2019 • Featured at Philadelphia Maker Faire
First Custom Robot • Autonomous & Mobile Platform

Zainiac 19 was Zain's first major robotics project, developed out of a drive to design and construct a functioning mobile robot from scratch. The build featured a custom chassis, motor driver integration, and ultrasonic obstacle avoidance. In 2019, Zain was selected as a featured maker at the Philadelphia Maker Faire, where he exhibited Zainiac 19 live to attendees in the Robotics category.

Joseph Henry Project

Summer 2025 • Princeton University
Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE)

During the summer of 2025, Zain participated as a high school research intern in the Joseph Henry Project within Princeton University's Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering under Professor Michael Littman. The project involves historical engineering reconstruction—studying and rebuilding the scientific electromechanical apparatus and motors invented by 19th-century physicist Joseph Henry, connecting historical physics principles with modern engineering concepts.

Volunteer Junior Firefighter

Active Service
Community Emergency Response & Apparatus Readiness

Zain serves as a junior volunteer firefighter, assisting in community emergency service and department readiness. Responsibilities and training include operating within the Incident Command System (ICS), training on fire service apparatus and tools, understanding water supply and hoseline deployment, and practicing safety procedures including Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) protocols.

This experience reinforces practical discipline, methodical safety checks, and steady communication in demanding, fast-paced environments.
