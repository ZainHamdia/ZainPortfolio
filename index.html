Full-Scale Humanoid (~5 Ft), LEGO MINDSTORMS & Kinematics

Conceived and fabricated a full-scale (~5 feet tall) humanoid robot synthesizing wood legs, a particle board and PVC pipe body, paper-mâché head, and custom 3D-printed parts with LEGO MINDSTORMS control—achieving mechanical kinematics with a panning neck, panning waist, and synchronized straight arms (no elbow bend) moving in opposite directions. Featured on Belgian national television news following participation in the Brussels Maker Faire.
