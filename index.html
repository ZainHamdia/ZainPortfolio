I build robots, mechanical controls, and hardware.

I’m a high school student in Princeton, New Jersey, planning to study mechanical engineering. Over the past seven years, I’ve built a five-foot humanoid robot out of wood and PVC, designed a 3D-printed animatronic head, fabricated flight simulator controls, built a wall-mounted Raspberry Pi dispatch display for my local firehouse, and spent a summer doing lab research at Princeton University.

Selected Projects & Roles

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2 (Station 46)

2025 – Present
Junior Firefighter & Technology Intern

Built a permanent Raspberry Pi dashboard for the station’s radio room showing live weather radar, alerts, and station info, housed inside a custom CAD-modeled and 3D-printed wall enclosure for an 11-inch screen. I also led the redesign of the fire company’s public website and helped revamp the Junior Firefighter onboarding and mentorship process.

The Zainiac Series (Zainiac 19, 21 & 25)

Brussels, Philadelphia, Rochester & Cairo Maker Faires
Independent Robotics Builds

What started in 2019 as a five-foot humanoid robot built with wood, PVC pipe, particle board, paper-mâché, 3D-printed brackets, and LEGO Mindstorms took me to Maker Faires in the US, Europe, and Egypt—including an appearance on Belgian national television news at the Brussels Maker Faire. The newest build, Zainiac 25, is an entirely 3D-printed animatronic head inspired by 1980s animatronics, using seven servos and linkage arms instead of pneumatic cylinders.

Princeton University Laboratory Learning Program

Summer 2025
Summer Research Intern — Joseph Henry Project

Spent the summer working in a Princeton University laboratory as part of the Joseph Henry Project, studying historical electromechanical instruments and contributing to hands-on physics and engineering research.
