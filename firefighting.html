MONTGOMERY TOWNSHIP VOLUNTEER FIRE COMPANY #2 • SUMMER 2026 – PRESENT

Technology & Engineering Intern

Radio Room Digital System, Custom CAD Enclosure & Website Redesign

In his role as Technology & Engineering Intern at Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2, Zain leads end-to-end hardware and software initiatives to enhance station operations and community engagement:

1. Raspberry Pi Digital Information Dashboard Designing and building a Raspberry Pi-based digital information system for permanent use in the station’s radio room. Integrates real-time operational information, live weather radar and forecasts, emergency alerts, news, and station communications into a centralized dashboard for firefighters.
2. Custom CAD & 3D-Printed Wall-Mounted Enclosure Engineering a custom wall-mounted enclosure for an 11-inch display and Raspberry Pi using CAD and 3D printing. Incorporates active ventilation, internal cable management, mounting hardware, and secure access to power and network connections.
3. Full Lifecycle Engineering Development Developing the system from initial requirements gathering and design through software/hardware integration, prototyping, reliability testing, physical installation, firefighter feedback, and technical documentation.
4. Department Public Website Redesign Leading the redesign and development of the fire company’s public website, transforming the existing site into a modern, accessible resource for department information, membership, community engagement, and recruitment.
