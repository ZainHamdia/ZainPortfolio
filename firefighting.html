Field Operations & Firefighter-Centered Design

How Active Training Shapes What I Build

Designing hardware and software for a firehouse requires understanding what happens when dispatch tones drop. Participating in weekly apparatus drills, SCBA evolutions, and live-burn training directly informs how I engineer tools for Station 46 crews.

Live-Burn & Smoke Tower Evolutions • Truck 46

Fireground Operations & Crew Coordination

Training at the county smoke house and burn building with Montgomery Truck 46 builds hands-on proficiency in hose advancement, aerial ladder support, and accountability under low-visibility conditions—teaching me how crews communicate and rely on clear situational data.

Station 46 Engine Bay • Rapid Turnout & SCBA

Designing for the 60-Second Dispatch Window

Gearing up in full PPE and SCBA in the apparatus bay means firefighters only have seconds to check weather hazards, road closures, and dispatch notes before rolling out. That firsthand constraint drove the zero-click, high-contrast layout of my 11-inch Radio Room Dashboard.
