Montgomery Fire Co. #2 • Station 46

Radio Room Display & Website Redesign

Technology & Engineering Intern
Summer 2026 – Present

As a Technology & Engineering Intern at Station 46, I took on two practical projects for the firehouse: building a permanent Raspberry Pi information display for the station’s radio room, and redesigning the fire company’s public website so community members and prospective volunteers can easily find what they need.

Wall-Mounted 11" Display & Raspberry Pi Enclosure CAD-Designed & 3D-Printed for the Station 46 Radio Room

Radio Room Hardware & Software

  • Centralized Dashboard: Runs on a Raspberry Pi to display live weather radar, forecasts, emergency alerts, news, and station updates in one place.
  • Custom 3D-Printed Enclosure: Modeled in CAD to hold the 11-inch screen and Raspberry Pi cleanly on the wall with built-in airflow vents, cable routing, and easy access to power and network ports.

Public Website Redesign

  • Clearer Community Resource: Rebuilt the department’s public website from the ground up so residents can quickly find department information and updates.
  • Recruitment & Membership: Made it much simpler for prospective volunteers and junior firefighters to learn how to join.
Emergency Service & Leadership

Junior Firefighter & Program Redesign

Montgomery Township Volunteer Fire Company #2
July 2025 – Present

Alongside my technical projects at the firehouse, I’m an active Junior Firefighter participating in drills, station operations, emergency calls, and community events.

I’m also leading an effort to improve our department’s Junior Firefighter Program—updating our online guides for new recruits, making the certification checklist clearer so Juniors know exactly what they need to respond to calls, and making it easier for newer members to connect with experienced mentors at the station.
