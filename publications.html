Published Articles in The Big Smoke

Digital Therapeutics & VR Medicine • 2022

Virtual Reality: Just What the Doctor Ordered

Traces the trajectory of virtual reality from early military flight simulators to cutting-edge medical analgesia, examining FDA EaseVRx clinical trials for chronic lower back pain, neurobiological distraction mechanisms in burn units, and the future of non-pharmacological hospital care.

Materials Science & Sustainability • 2022

The Clandestine Side of 3D Printing

Examines how additive manufacturing is revolutionizing global supply chains while confronting packaging waste, exploring water-soluble polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) support filament, water-activated dissolution, and closed-loop polymer recycling that eliminates wasteful cardboard boxes.

Neuroscience & Virtual Environments • 2022

Have We Been Here Before?

Investigates the cognitive neuroscience and perceptual pathways behind déjà vu, examining dual-neurological processing desynchronization, temporal lobe familiarity circuits, and laboratory virtual reality experiments manipulating spatial recognition cues.

Robotics Lineage & Maker Faires • 2021

From COVID-19 to Zainiac 19

Chronicles the design inspiration (rooted in Five Nights at Freddy's animatronics), full-scale construction, and international demonstrations of the 5-foot humanoid Zainiac 19 across Maker Faires in Brussels, Rochester, Cairo, and Philadelphia, and how pandemic shutdowns halted Zainiac 20 before inspiring Zainiac 21.
