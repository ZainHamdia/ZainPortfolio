Travel Highlights & Cultural Notes

Europe • 7 Destinations

Western & Southern Europe

Exhibited the ~5-foot humanoid robot Zainiac 19 at the Brussels Maker Faire (featured on Belgian National TV News and welcomed at the official opening ceremony). Explored classical structural engineering across Italy (Milan, Florence, Pisa, Venice), cultural institutions in London and Paris, and the historic crossroads of Sarajevo.

Brussels Maker Faire Belgian National TV Italian Architecture Sarajevo History
Asia • 3 Destinations

Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto, Kanazawa)

Explored the convergence of advanced robotics technology, automated high-speed transit networks (Shinkansen), and centuries-old architectural heritage—from traditional Japanese master timber carpentry without nails in Kyoto to samurai district preservation and modern art curation in Kanazawa.

Robotics Innovation Transit Engineering Timber Joinery Contemporary Art
Middle East & Africa • 2 Destinations

Egypt (Cairo & El Gouna)

Presented Zainiac 19 at the Cairo Maker Faire at the Smart Village youth robotics exchange. In El Gouna along the Red Sea coast, studied eco-sustainable lagoon community planning, renewable energy infrastructure, and coastal marine engineering.

Cairo Maker Faire Ancient Engineering Red Sea Coast Lagoon Architecture
Americas • 1 Destination

Costa Rica (Orosi Valley)

Visited the lush volcanic river basin of the Orosi Valley in Cartago Province, exploring colonial architectural landmarks including the Iglesia de San José de Orosi (built 1743), thermal waters, geothermal geology, and sustainable shade-grown coffee cultivation.

Cartago Province Colonial Heritage Volcanic Geology Biodiversity
