3D-Printed Flight Simulator

Custom-Designed & 3D-Printed Mechanical Components

Zain designed and constructed a custom flight simulator incorporating original CAD-modeled and 3D-printed physical components.

The project involved engineering functional physical flight controls, designing mechanical assemblies to translate human physical inputs into simulator commands, and iterating on structural tolerances and ergonomics through 3D printing.

Princeton University Summer Research Intern

Joseph Henry Project • Summer 2025
Program Summary: During the summer of 2025, Zain served as a summer research intern in Princeton University's Laboratory Learning Program, participating in the Joseph Henry Project research program.

In this role, Zain conducted scientific research and contributed directly to academic project work, studying historical electromechanical apparatus and scientific experimental design in a formal university laboratory setting.
