Custom-Designed & 3D-Printed Mechanical Assemblies & Physical Flight Controls

Zain conceived, designed, and constructed a custom flight simulator incorporating original CAD-modeled and 3D-printed physical components. The project involved engineering functional physical flight controls, designing mechanical assemblies to translate physical pilot inputs into simulation commands, and iterating on structural tolerances and ergonomics through 3D printing.

Connecting Aeronautics, Additive Manufacturing & Electromechanics

Building physical flight simulation hardware requires solving foundational mechanical engineering challenges: calculating bearing loads, managing structural elasticity under dynamic pilot forces, and establishing consistent electrical signals from mechanical linkages. Paired with Princeton University's formal electromechanical laboratory research, this work reinforces Zain's rigorous technical foundation as an aspiring mechanical engineer.
