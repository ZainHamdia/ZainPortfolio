02 / Flight Simulation & Laboratory Work

Aviation & Research

Designing and 3D-printing physical flight simulator controls at home, paired with summer research in Princeton University’s Laboratory Learning Program.

CAD & Additive Manufacturing

3D-Printed Flight Simulator

Custom mechanical controls designed in CAD and printed from scratch
Personal Build

Commercial flight simulator hardware is expensive, so I decided to design and 3D-print my own flight controls. The project meant figuring out how to turn smooth physical pilot inputs—pitch, roll, and throttle movement—into reliable mechanical linkages that don’t bind or flex under load, and iterating on part tolerances until everything fit together cleanly.

University Research

Princeton University Laboratory Learning Program

Summer Research Intern • The Joseph Henry Project
Summer 2025

During the summer of 2025, I worked as a research intern in Princeton University’s Laboratory Learning Program as part of the Joseph Henry Project. Working inside a university physics and engineering lab gave me the chance to study early electromechanical instruments, run hands-on experiments, and see how careful measurement and mechanical design work together in academic research.
