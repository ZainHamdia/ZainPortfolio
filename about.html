Background & Academic Focus

Zain Hamdia is a senior at Princeton Day School in Princeton, New Jersey, preparing to pursue undergraduate studies in Aerospace Engineering. His technical trajectory is defined by hands-on fabrication, laboratory research, and civic service.

His engineering work started with building custom robotics—most notably his first robot, Zainiac 19, which earned selection as an official maker exhibit at the 2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire. Since then, he has expanded his prototyping work in the school's STEAM Lab, conducted collegiate electromechanical research in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Princeton University, and trained as a volunteer junior firefighter in his community.

Chronology

Engineering & Service Milestones

Key steps shaping Zain's growth as an engineer and community volunteer.

2019
Zainiac 19 & Philadelphia Maker Faire

Constructed first mobile robot, Zainiac 19, equipped with obstacle detection. Selected as an official maker exhibit at the 2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire in the Robotics category, demonstrating the build to attendees.

2023 - 2024
STEAM Lab Expansion & Advanced Prototyping

Deepened expertise in 3D parametric CAD modeling and additive manufacturing at Princeton Day School's STEAM Lab, designing functional custom mechanical parts, motor mounts, and brackets.

2024 - PRESENT
Volunteer Junior Firefighter Service

Joined the local volunteer fire department in a junior capacity, training on fireground apparatus, hydraulics, breathing apparatus safety (SCBA), and emergency command coordination.

SUMMER 2025
Princeton University MAE Research Internship

Conducted research as a high school intern in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Princeton University on the Joseph Henry Project under Prof. Michael Littman, studying historic electromechanical apparatus and electromagnetism.

2025 - 2026
High School Senior & Aerospace Engineering Aspirations

Completing senior year at Princeton Day School, synthesizing experiences in robotics, aerospace research, and firefighting in preparation for university engineering studies.
