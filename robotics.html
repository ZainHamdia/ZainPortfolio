Zainiac Robot Series: Generations 19 • 21 • 25

Active Platform Lineage
Mechanical Chassis Design, Microcontroller Architecture, Sensory Integration & Iterative Prototyping

The Zainiac Robot Series represents an ongoing lineage of original mobile robots conceived, designed, and fabricated independently by Zain Hamdia. Starting with his landmark first build, Zainiac 19, the platform explores the synthesis of mechanical frame architecture, self-taught Arduino embedded programming in C++, dual motor driver electronics, and sensory obstacle avoidance.

// CAD SCHEMATIC: DIFFERENTIAL DRIVE ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION ZAINIAC PLATFORM • REV 3.2 • SCALE 1:2
// GENERATION 1 ARCHITECTURE: ZAINIAC 19 (DEBUT: 2019)
First independent robotics build. Conceived and fabricated from the ground up, featuring self-taught Arduino programming in C++, dual motor driver circuitry, differential chassis assembly, and ultrasonic distance telemetry. Debuted publicly at the Philadelphia and Brussels Maker Faires.

// COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE FABRICATION & RESEARCH

3D-Printed N95 Masks (COVID-19 Relief)

Manufactured For & Donated Directly to New York Cancer Center

During the severe personal protective equipment shortages of the COVID-19 pandemic, Zain designed and manufactured 3D-printed N95 masks using rapid FDM additive manufacturing, donating them directly to a New York cancer center to protect vulnerable oncology patients and healthcare staff.

// HEALTHCARE IMPACT & PUBLISHED RESEARCH
Donated directly to frontline hospital oncology wards during critical supply-chain disruption. This humanitarian fabrication initiative directly inspired his published article: “From COVID-19 to Zainiac 19”.
