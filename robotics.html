Servos & Linkage Arms
Replaces 1980s pneumatic cylinders

This is what my robot was based on: a head of an animatronic from the 1980s that used pneumatics as they were cheap and easy to set up. Mine was entirely 3D printed, and used servo motors for movement and linkage arms as opposed to pneumatic cylinders.

How Zainiac 25 Works

From 1980s Pneumatics to 3D-Printed Servo Linkages

Instead of metal tubing and compressed air cylinders, I modeled and 3D-printed the entire head structure and linkage arms so seven hobby servos could drive every axis cleanly:

  • 2 Servos at the Base of the Neck: Pitch and tilt the head assembly.
  • 2 Servos to Move Each Eye: Independent pan and tilt beneath black eyelids.
  • 1 Servo to Move the Jaw: Pulls the linkage arm attached to the lower mandible.
