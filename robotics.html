Zainiac 19: First Custom Mobile Robot

2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire (Robotics Category)

Zainiac 19 marks the inception of Zain Hamdia's hands-on engineering path. Driven by a desire to understand how code directly commands mechanical motion in the real world, Zain designed, wired, and assembled this mobile robot from the ground up.

// CORE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE:
• Microcontroller Logic: Embedded processing executing real-time distance scans
• Sensing: Forward ultrasonic sonar transducers measuring spatial clearances
• Drivetrain & Actuation: Dual geared DC motors with integrated motor driver circuitry
• Platform: Custom fabricated multi-layer chassis with balanced center-of-gravity

The robot operates autonomously by continuously scanning its surroundings, interpreting distance feedback, and executing avoidance turns when approaching obstacles.

Live Public Exhibition: Zain was selected to present Zainiac 19 at the 2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire. Exhibiting in the Robotics category, he operated live technical demonstrations for attendees, walking engineers, teachers, and students through the hardware layout, wiring schematics, and obstacle navigation code.

STEAM Lab CAD Modeling & 3D Prototyping

Design for Additive Manufacturing & Mechanical Fit

At Princeton Day School, Zain actively utilizes the campus STEAM Lab facilities and his personal workbench to engineer custom parts for physical assemblies. Rather than adapting pre-existing generic brackets, he creates custom parametric geometries in CAD software.

// FABRICATION WORKFLOW:
• Parametric 3D Modeling: Onshape & Fusion 360
• Additive Manufacturing: Slicing optimization, infill load direction, and perimeter tuning
• Prototyping Cycle: CAD Model → FDM Print → Caliper Measurement → Iteration

Projects range from motor mounts and vibration dampening brackets to custom enclosures and mechanical linkages. By studying layer adhesion orientation and physical clearance tolerances (interference vs. sliding fits), he ensures every printed part performs reliably under mechanical load.
