Zainiac Robot Series: Generations 19 • 21 • 25

Active Platform Lineage
Full-Scale Humanoid (~5 Ft), Multi-Material Fabrication, LEGO MINDSTORMS & Kinematics

The Zainiac Robot Series represents an ongoing lineage of original robots conceived, designed, and constructed independently by Zain Hamdia. His landmark first build, Zainiac 19, is a full-scale humanoid robot standing approximately 5 feet tall. Conceived and constructed using an authentic synthesis of rapid fabrication materials: engineered with wood for the legs, a particle board and PVC pipe framework for the body, a sculpted paper-mâché head, and custom 3D-printed parts. Powered by LEGO MINDSTORMS, Zainiac 19 features physical mechanical kinematics engineered so that its neck pans only, its waist pans only, and its straight arms (elbows do not bend) move in synchronized movement with each arm moving in the opposite direction.

// CAD SCHEMATIC: ZAINIAC 25 ENTIRELY 3D-PRINTED ANIMATRONIC HEAD BASED ON 1980s KINEMATICS • SERVO MOTORS & LINKAGE ARMS • REV 4.0
// DESIGN INSPIRATION: 1980s PNEUMATIC ANIMATRONIC HEAD
This is what my robot was based on: a head of an animatronic from the 1980s that used pneumatics as they were cheap and easy to set up. Mine was entirely 3D printed, and used servo motors for movement and linkage arms as opposed to pneumatic cylinders.
// GENERATION 3 ARCHITECTURE: 3D-PRINTED ELECTROMECHANICAL UPGRADE
While original 1980s animatronic heads relied on pneumatic cylinders because pneumatics were economical and simple to implement in that era, Zainiac 25 was entirely 3D printed and translated the geometry into an electromechanical system driven by servo motors and linkage arms:
  • Entirely 3D Printed: All cranial cage components, eye gimbals, neck base mounts, and linkage arms were custom 3D-printed.
  • 2 Servos at Base of Neck: Drive articulated multi-axis head pitch (nod) and tilt/roll movement via mechanical linkage arms.
  • 2 Servos to Move Each Eye: Provide responsive, independent pan and tilt gaze tracking beneath black eyelids.
  • 1 Dedicated Servo to Move Jaw: Direct linkage arm actuates the central mandibular pivot and D-shaped jaw plate for speech timing.
// ELECTROMECHANICAL EVOLUTION: 7 SERVOS & LINKAGE ARMS //

3D-Printed N95 Masks (COVID-19 Relief)

Manufactured For & Donated Directly to New York Cancer Center

During the severe personal protective equipment shortages of the COVID-19 pandemic, Zain designed and manufactured 3D-printed N95 masks using rapid FDM additive manufacturing, donating them directly to a New York cancer center to protect vulnerable oncology patients and healthcare staff.
