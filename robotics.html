Zainiac Robot Series

Mechanical Design, Electronics, Programming & Iterative Prototyping

The Zainiac Robot Series represents an ongoing lineage of original mobile robots conceived, designed, and constructed independently by Zain Hamdia. Starting with his landmark first build, Zainiac 19, the series incorporates mechanical design, electronics, self-taught Arduino programming, fabrication, and iterative prototyping.

// CORE ENGINEERING DISCIPLINES:
• Mechanical Design: Custom chassis layouts, drivetrain assembly, and structural framing.
• Electronics & Control: Self-taught Arduino programming, sensor integration, and motor driver wiring.
• Iterative Prototyping: Evolution through successive generations (Zainiac 19 → Zainiac 21 → Zainiac 25).

International Exhibitions & Recognition: The Zainiac series has been presented at prominent maker events internationally and across the United States:

  • Brussels Maker Faire: Featured on Belgian national news following participation and invited to participate in the opening ceremony alongside Brussels government leadership.
  • Philadelphia Maker Faire: Featured maker in the Robotics category.
  • Rochester Maker Faire
  • Cairo Maker Faire
  • Tandem Industrial Design Conference at Jefferson University

COMPETITIVE ROBOTICS • PRINCETON DAY SCHOOL

VEX V5 Competitive Robotics & Club Leadership

Co-President (2025–Present) • Head of Design Team (2024–2025)

Zain serves as Co-President of the Princeton Day School Robotics Club (2025–Present) and previously served as Head of the Design Team (2024–2025). In these leadership roles, he directs CAD modeling, mechanism selection, and structural fabrication under strict competition constraints.

COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE FABRICATION

3D-Printed N95 Masks (COVID-19 Relief)

Donated to New York Cancer Center

During the height of personal protective equipment shortages during the COVID-19 pandemic, Zain designed and produced 3D-printed N95 masks, donating them directly to a New York cancer center to protect vulnerable patients and medical staff.

This work inspired his published article: “From COVID-19 to Zainiac 19”.
