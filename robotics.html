Zainiac 19: First Custom Mobile Robot

2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire (Robotics Category)

Zainiac 19 represents the beginning of Zain Hamdia's hands-on engineering journey. Motivated by a curiosity about how physical mechanisms and electronic control systems interact, Zain set out to design and construct a functioning mobile robot from scratch.

The robot was built as a multi-functional mobile platform with integrated motor drives and forward-facing ultrasonic sonar sensing to detect obstacles and navigate its surroundings autonomously. Through iterative testing, Zain wired the power distribution, connected motor driver circuitry, and programmed the microcontroller logic to make decisions based on real-time distance measurements.

The 2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire: Zainiac 19 was selected as an official exhibit at the 2019 Philadelphia Maker Faire in the Robotics category. Zain showcased the robot live to thousands of attendees, explaining the chassis assembly, wiring, and obstacle avoidance logic to fellow makers, engineers, and curious visitors.

This early experience taught essential engineering fundamentals: the importance of weight balance, mechanical durability under repeated operation, and the ability to clearly explain complex technical ideas to an audience.

2023 - PRESENT • PRINCETON DAY SCHOOL

STEAM Lab CAD Modeling & 3D Prototyping

Design for Additive Manufacturing & Mechanical Assembly

At Princeton Day School, Zain actively utilizes the campus STEAM Lab and his home workshop to transform ideas into physical hardware. Rather than relying solely on pre-fabricated parts, he designs custom components using 3D CAD modeling software (such as Onshape and Fusion 360) and manufactures them using 3D printers.

His work focuses on functional, end-use components—including motor brackets, sensor mountings, alignment jigs, and custom enclosures. By mastering print orientations, slicing settings, and geometric tolerances, Zain ensures that 3D-printed parts can withstand mechanical loads and vibration.

This rapid prototyping loop allows for quick iteration: designing a concept in CAD, fabricating a test specimen, measuring physical fit with calipers, and refining the geometry within hours.
